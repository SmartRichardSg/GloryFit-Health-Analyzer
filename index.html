GloryFit Health Analyzer

Understand your trends. One step at a time.

For personal tracking, not diagnosis. GloryFit measurements and sleep-stage/stress estimates can be inaccurate. This app processes imported files in your browser; it does not send them to a health service. Seek medical advice for concerns and urgent care for severe symptoms.

1. Load your GloryFit data

Choose the HealthDataExport.zip file from the iPhone Files app. The app reads the five CSV files inside it. You can import a newer export later to update your history.
